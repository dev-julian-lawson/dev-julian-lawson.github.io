Privacy Policy for Just Another Pointless Puzzle

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Julian Lawson ("we") makes Just Another Pointless Puzzle (the "game"). This policy explains what the game collects and why. Contact: dev.julian.lawson@proton.me.

What we collect

  • Gameplay and progress. Level progress, settings, gems and unlocked packs are stored on your device. If you are signed in, they are also synced to our game backend so you can restore them.
  • Player identifier. A player id (through Google Play Games sign-in, or a random id generated by the game) so your progress and leaderboard entries belong to you.
  • Usage analytics. Events such as starting or finishing a level, session length, game version and platform. We use these to balance levels and fix problems.
  • Leaderboards. Your leaderboard times and display name are visible to other players.
  • Ads. The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos or files. We do not sell your data.

Service providers

Game backend and analytics: Talo (https://trytalo.com). Ads: Google AdMob. Purchases and sign-in: Google Play. These providers process data on our behalf under their own policies.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

We keep synced progress and analytics while your player record exists. To delete your server-side data, email dev.julian.lawson@proton.me from the account or and include the game name (we may ask for your Play Games player name to find your record). Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
