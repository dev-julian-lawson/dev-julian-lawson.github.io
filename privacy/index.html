Privacy Policy for Just Another Pointless Puzzle Game 3D

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Sleeperverse Studio operates Just Another Pointless Puzzle Game 3D (the “game”). This policy explains how information is used when you play, sign in, view ads, buy packs, or delete your game account. Contact us at sleeperversestudio@gmail.com.

Information used by the game

  • Local play. The game keeps progress, gems and gem transactions, earned unlocks, tutorial state, preferences, and pending scores on your device. You can play as a guest without signing in.
  • Google Play Games sign-in. If you sign in, Google Play Games supplies a player identity and may supply your gamer name and profile image. The game uses a short-lived server authorization code to identify your account with Talo. We do not ask for your Google password or your account email address. Your gamer name and image can appear in your in-game profile.
  • Cloud saves and leaderboards. Talo stores a player record and synced progress, completed levels, scores, best times, gems and gem transactions, earned unlocks, and related game activity for a signed-in player. Leaderboard entries, including your gamer name, score and time, may be visible to other players. Local saves are associated with the signed-in player so progress from different accounts is kept separate.
  • Gameplay analytics. For signed-in players, Talo receives game events such as game and session starts, level starts and completions, restarts, daily challenges, scores, level or mode identifiers, app version, and platform. We use these events to operate and improve the game. Events generated during guest play may be sent if that guest progress is later claimed by the first account that signs in on the device.
  • Purchases. Google Play handles purchases and payment details. The game checks purchases through Google Play to grant or restore pack access. Purchase entitlement follows the Google Play store account used on the device and is separate from your Play Games progress account. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Optional rewarded ads. The game uses Google AdMob and its consent platform. The ads SDK may process information including your IP address, advertising or other device identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostics to provide and measure ads, subject to your applicable privacy choices. The game checks whether the consent platform permits ad requests before loading ads. You can review available choices in Settings → About → Ad privacy options when that option appears, and manage your advertising ID in Android settings.

The game does not ask you to enter your legal name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or files. Your Play Games profile can include a gamer name and image; the game and its service providers also process the identifiers and technical data described above. We do not sell player data.

Services involved

Google Play Games provides sign-in and profile information; Google Play processes purchases; Google AdMob and its partners serve and measure optional ads; Talo provides player records, cloud saves, leaderboards, and game analytics; and our Cloudflare account-deletion service verifies requests and coordinates deletion. These services may process data under their own policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy, Talo, and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Signed-in game records remain available while the account exists. To delete your game account in the app, open Profile → Delete account, confirm, and verify with the owning Google Play Games profile. This deletes the matching Talo player record, cloud save, and leaderboard entries and clears that account’s local data on the device once deletion is confirmed. A later sign-in with the same Play Games profile can create a new, empty game account. Deleting the game account does not delete your Google account, Play Games profile, or Google Play purchases.

The public account-deletion instructions explain the process. If you no longer have the app, email sleeperversestudio@gmail.com to request deletion. We will verify ownership before deleting an account. The Cloudflare service processes request IP addresses for short-lived rate limiting and retains a minimal deletion-operation record for 30 days after completion or failure, then removes it. Copies of local progress stored offline on other devices cannot be erased remotely. Uninstalling the game removes data on that device but does not by itself delete a cloud account.

If you are playing as a guest, Profile → Reset guest progress clears that device’s current guest progress. It does not delete another signed-in account or store purchases. You can also contact us about privacy or deletion at the email above.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you believe a younger child’s information was collected, contact us so we can review and address it.

Security and changes

The game uses encrypted connections when it communicates with online services. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. We will post policy changes here and update the date at the top.
